WHO WE ARE

A professional support system for your loan journey.

Financial decisions can be important and sometimes complex. MN TECH FINSOLUTIONS is built to make the enquiry journey more organised and understandable.

Our role begins with listening to the customer's requirement. We help identify the relevant loan category, explain the typical information and documents that may be required, and coordinate the enquiry with appropriate lending institutions where applicable.

We do not believe in creating unrealistic expectations. Every application is ultimately assessed by the respective bank or NBFC under its own credit, eligibility, documentation and underwriting policies.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions customers commonly have

Is MN Tech Finsolutions a bank or NBFC?

No. MN Tech Finsolutions provides loan sourcing, facilitation and customer-support services. Lending and sanction decisions are made by the respective bank or NBFC.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee approval?

No. Approval depends on the respective lender's eligibility, credit assessment, documentation and internal policies.

Can you assist with different types of loans?

Yes. Our website covers several personal and business finance categories. Availability depends on customer profile, location and current lender arrangements.

Who decides the interest rate and loan amount?

The respective lender determines the final rate, fees, sanctioned amount, tenure and other terms after assessment.

What documents will I need?

The exact list varies. Typical categories can include KYC, income or business proof, banking records and product-specific documents. The lender confirms the final checklist.
